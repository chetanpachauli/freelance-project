# Marketing Talk — Premium Video Editing Landing Page

A high-converting, cinematic sales landing page designed for **Marketing Talk Post-Production Studio** to convert foreign prospects across Instagram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Email outreach into qualified client quotes.

## ?? Key Features

- **Dark & Cinematic Luxury Design**: Tailored for international agency standards with amber/gold accent lighting and clean typography.
- **Three.js 3D Hero Canvas**: Interactive ambient particles and rotating 3D wireframe mesh.
- **Interactive Before & After Split-Screen Slider**: Real-time draggable comparison between flat camera RAW and 4K color-graded master edits.
- **Interactive Deliverable & ROI Capacity Calculator**: Dynamic monthly video slider (4–48 videos) auto-calculating hours saved, dedicated editors, and turnaround time.
- **Multi-Industry Categorized Portfolio**: Filter tabs (Real Estate, Personal Branding, Finance, Healthcare, Education, E-commerce) with full-screen 4K playable video modal.
- **4-Step Holographic Pipeline**: 3D mega icon badges, animated neon connecting laser line, step pills, and micro-deliverable chips.
- **Staggered Scroll-Reveal Animations**: Fluid cascading entrance as users scroll down the page.
- **Floating Side Navigation Indicator**: Right-edge vertical dot indicator tracking active sections with hover tooltips and one-click smooth scrolling.
- **100% Mobile Responsive**: Mobile-first architecture with persistent bottom quick-action quote CTA.

## ??? Tech Stack

- **Vite** (Build Tool & Bundler)
- **Tailwind CSS** (Utility-first Modern Styling)
- **Three.js** (WebGL 3D Interactive Mesh)
- **Lucide Icons**
- **Vanilla JavaScript ES Modules**
